Capture a product page from your browser

Some suppliers — Sigma-Aldrich and R&D Systems among them — refuse requests from this server, and others build their pages in JavaScript so there is nothing to read. Your browser can already see those pages, so this hands the page over from there.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

📥 Lab Capture

Dragging it is the point — clicking it here does nothing useful.

Open the supplier's product page in a normal tab.

Click Lab Capture in your bookmarks bar. It will confirm the page was sent.

Come back here, open a record, and press From browser next to the address box.

The bookmark only sends the page you are looking at, to {{ request.host_url }}. It keeps one page at a time, holds it in memory rather than on disk, and changes no records by itself.

If your bookmarks bar is hidden, press Ctrl+Shift+B.
